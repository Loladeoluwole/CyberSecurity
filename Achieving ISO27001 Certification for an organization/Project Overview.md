# Project Overview

In this project, I will build an Information Security Management System (ISMS) from scratch for Stark Industries Inc. a small USA based software company developing a SaaS marketing application, using ISO/IEC 27001:2022 as the framework.

The company aims to achieve ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, with the SaaS product included in the ISMS scope, to support sales and strengthen customer trust.

## Company Description

- **Name:** Stark Industries Inc.
- **Location:** New York City, New York.
- **Product:** A SaaS platform offering marketing automation tools for small businesses, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).
- **Scope:** The development, deployment, and management of the Stark Industries SaaS platform.
- **Customer Demand:** Prospective clients require ISO 27001 certification as a condition for procurement.
- **Stakeholders:** Information Security Manager, Information Security Analyst, IT Infrastructure Engineer, Software Developer, and Amazon Web Services (AWS).

## My Role

I have been hired by the Information Security Manager as a Cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Analyst to develop the ISMS and support the organisation’s preparation for ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification.

The ISMS scope is limited to the newly developed SaaS application and the activities supporting its development, deployment, and management.
